## About me

I'm a **Front-End Developer specialised in React and TypeScript**, with a background combining **technology, business analysis, data and sport**.

Before becoming a developer, I worked for several years as a **Business Analyst in the sports retail and distribution industry**. This experience gave me a strong understanding of business challenges, data and product requirements.

I then transitioned into software development and worked for a French **rugby statistics and performance technology company**, building products around sports data and performance.

Today, I work as a Front-End Developer, building complex web applications and working with modern technologies such as **React, TypeScript and data visualisation**.

My background allows me to look at a product from different perspectives:

**Business → Data → UX → Technology**

I enjoy turning complex problems and data into **simple, intuitive and scalable interfaces**.

## A few things about me

### 🏃‍♀️ Sport is a big part of my life

I've been involved in competitive sport since I was 14.

I was a **high-level handball player** and was selected for the **French Junior National Team in 2009**.

Today, I continue to train and compete, particularly in **triathlon and cycling**, alongside surfing and CrossFit.

Sport has taught me a lot about **discipline, performance, consistency and teamwork** — values that I also bring into my work as a developer.

### 📊 I like working with data

I'm particularly interested in the challenge of taking **complex datasets and turning them into something people can actually understand and use**.

Good data visualisation isn't just about displaying numbers.

It's about helping users **see patterns, understand situations and make better decisions**.

### 🧠 I think beyond the code

Coming from a Business Analyst background, I naturally think about:

**Why are we building this?**
**Who is going to use it?**
**What problem are we solving?**
**How can we make the experience simpler?**

For me, writing good code is only part of building a good product.
